About the Voice Agent

A real-time voice assistant that lives entirely in your browser. You talk, it thinks, and it replies out loud.

What it is

This is a hands-free conversational interface. There are no buttons and no menus — you just talk. Your microphone feeds a stream straight to a server that transcribes you, reasons about what you said (including querying live tools), and speaks the answer back in a natural voice.

How to use it

  • Start/stop speaking: press space.
  • Cut it off mid-answer: press space to interrupt.
  • Transcripts stream in the center of the screen — your words appear right-aligned in green, its answers left-aligned in white.

The visualizer

Green bars — your voice being heard.
White bars — the assistant speaking.
Dim bars — it's thinking / processing.

When things go quiet, the bars settle flat and the line fades away after a moment until you speak again.

How it works

Your microphone→ Moonshine streaming STT→ Groq qwen3.6-27b→ Live tools→ Piper TTS→ Played & visualized

Audio is captured at 16 kHz mono, sent as a continuous stream over a secure WebSocket, and transcribed live while you talk. The language model decides whether to call a tool to get real, current answers.

Capabilities

  • Weather — live conditions for any location.
  • Calculator — arithmetic and expressions.
  • Wikipedia — look up facts and articles.
  • Currency conversion — live exchange rates.
  • World time — current time anywhere on Earth.
  • Dictionary — word definitions and meanings.

Stack

Frontend: a single self-contained page — HTML, CSS, and the Web Audio API. Backend: FastAPI WebSocket service behind nginx. All upstream APIs used by the tools are free and keyless.
